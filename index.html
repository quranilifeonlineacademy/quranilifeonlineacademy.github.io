      Important guidelines for teachers

      Dear teacher, as a respected member of Qurani Life Online Academy, you are requested to follow these important rules.

      • Take every class at the scheduled time.
      • Keep good, Islamic manners with every student.
      • Teach with correct (sahih) recitation and Tajweed.

      • Students and teachers must not exchange any personal information with each other.
      • Do not settle any academy-related problem on your own without consulting the authority.

        Light of the Qur'an

        Why learning the Qur'an matters so much

        وَلَقَدْ يَسَّرْنَا الْقُرْآنَ لِلذِّكْرِ فَهَلْ مِن مُّدَّكِرٍ

        “And We have certainly made the Qur'an easy to remember. So is there anyone who will be mindful?”

        Surah Al-Qamar, 54:17
        The best of people

        خَيْرُكُمْ مَنْ تَعَلَّمَ الْقُرْآنَ وَعَلَّمَهُ

        “The best of you are those who learn the Qur'an and teach it.”

        Narrated by Uthman ibn Affan (RA) · Sahih al-Bukhari 5027
        Ten rewards for every letter

        مَنْ قَرَأَ حَرْفًا مِنْ كِتَابِ اللَّهِ فَلَهُ بِهِ حَسَنَةٌ وَالْحَسَنَةُ بِعَشْرِ أَمْثَالِهَا

        “Whoever reads a letter from the Book of Allah earns a good deed, and each good deed is multiplied ten times.”

        Narrated by Abdullah ibn Mas'ud (RA) · Jami at-Tirmidhi 2910
        The virtue of Surah Al-BaqarahBlessing, intercession and protection from sorcery

        اقْرَءُوا الْقُرْآنَ فَإِنَّهُ يَأْتِي يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ شَفِيعًا لِأَصْحَابِهِ ... اقْرَءُوا سُورَةَ الْبَقَرَةِ، فَإِنَّ أَخْذَهَا بَرَكَةٌ، وَتَرْكَهَا حَسْرَةٌ، وَلَا تَسْتَطِيعُهَا الْبَطَلَةُ

        The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: “Recite the Qur'an, for it will come on the Day of Resurrection as an intercessor for its companions. … Recite Surah Al-Baqarah. Holding to it is a blessing, leaving it is a regret, and the people of falsehood (al-batalah) cannot overcome it.”

        Narrated by Abu Umamah al-Bahili (RA) · Sahih Muslim 804
        What does “al-batalah” mean?Mu'awiyah ibn Sallam, a narrator of this hadith, said: “It has reached me that al-batalah means the sorcerers.” In other words, sorcery and black magic cannot overpower the one who keeps reciting Surah Al-Baqarah, in shaa Allah.

        لَا تَجْعَلُوا بُيُوتَكُمْ مَقَابِرَ، إِنَّ الشَّيْطَانَ يَنْفِرُ مِنَ الْبَيْتِ الَّذِي تُقْرَأُ فِيهِ سُورَةُ الْبَقَرَةِ

        “Do not turn your homes into graves. Shaytan flees from the house in which Surah Al-Baqarah is recited.”

        Narrated by Abu Hurayrah (RA) · Sahih Muslim 780
        The ten verses

        The Companion Abdullah ibn Mas'ud (RA) said that on the night these ten verses of Surah Al-Baqarah are recited, Shaytan does not enter that home: the first four, Ayat al-Kursi with the two after it, and the last three.

        Sunan ad-Darimi 3425 (saying of a Companion)
        The cost of staying away from the Qur'an

        وَقَالَ الرَّسُولُ يَا رَبِّ إِنَّ قَوْمِي اتَّخَذُوا هَٰذَا الْقُرْآنَ مَهْجُورًا

        “And the Messenger will say: O my Lord, my people took this Qur'an as something abandoned.”

        Surah Al-Furqan, 25:30

        وَمَنْ أَعْرَضَ عَن ذِكْرِي فَإِنَّ لَهُ مَعِيشَةً ضَنكًا

        “Whoever turns away from My remembrance will have a life of hardship.”

        Surah Ta-Ha, 20:124

        If we cannot read Surah Al-Baqarah, Ayat al-Kursi and its closing verses correctly, how will we receive this protection and blessing? Learning the Qur'an correctly, for ourselves and our children, is a duty on each of us.
